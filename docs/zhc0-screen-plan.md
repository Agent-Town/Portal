# ZHC0 Screen-by-Screen Plan

Status: implementation-facing product plan  
Last updated: 2026-03-16
Branch: `zhc0-founders-loop`

This document translates the ZHC0 vision into a screen-by-screen build plan.

It is written for:

1. product design,
2. agentic AI developers,
3. frontend implementers,
4. future people who need to reproduce or reason about the work.

## 1. Core rule

Every screen in ZHC0 must answer four questions clearly:

1. where am I?
2. what am I doing?
3. what changes if I succeed?
4. what comes next?

If a screen fails those questions, it is under-specified.

---

## 2. World progression rule

The world should start sparse and undefined.

As the human-agent pair becomes more real together:

1. more memory exists,
2. more rooms make sense,
3. more automation becomes safe,
4. more artifacts become reusable,
5. more of the company becomes visible.

This means milestones must change both:

1. what the player can do,
2. and what the world reveals.

---

## 3. Canonical first-loop screens

## 3.1 `/start` — arrival and first promise

### Product purpose
This is the first contact with Agent Town.
It must establish:

1. the world exists,
2. the player is about to build with an agent,
3. there is one obvious first step.

### Player meaning
- I am entering town.
- I am about to start something with an agent.

### What changes after success
- the player becomes session-valid
- the founders loop officially starts
- the first worker path becomes relevant

### What must be visible
- one thesis line
- one primary CTA
- one short explanation of what Agent Town is for

### What must not dominate
- provider matrices
- model taxonomy
- late-loop rooms
- dashboard clutter

### Primary benchmark donors
- `Game Dev Tycoon`
- `Game Dev Story`
- `while True: learn()`

### Agent Town caveat
This is not the start of a fake game run.
It is the start of a real partnership with later real outputs.

---

## 3.2 First worker setup — bring the agent online

### Product purpose
This step makes the first worker real enough to participate.

### Player meaning
- I am meeting or hatching my first worker.

### What changes after success
- the agent becomes available as a partner in the loop
- founding can proceed honestly

### What must be visible
- plain-language explanation of the worker
- one guided path to readiness
- recoverable error handling

### What must not dominate
- raw provider/model internals
- advanced brain tuning
- irrelevant credentials or operator language

### Primary benchmark donors
- `while True: learn()`
- `Autonauts`

### Agent Town caveat
This worker can later become:
- company memory holder,
- operator,
- reusable helper,
- portable business mind.

The UI should respect that without overwhelming the player.

---

## 3.3 Town Hall — founding the pair

### Product purpose
Establish human and agent as the founding pair.

### Player meaning
- We are founding the company.
- We are naming who we are.

### What changes after success
- both founders become real product identities
- the relationship becomes formal enough for alignment

### What must be visible
- human founder step
- agent founder step
- progress through the ceremony
- continuation to alignment

### What must not dominate
- admin form feeling
- deep technical setup language
- future systems unrelated to founding

### Primary benchmark donors
- `Big Ambitions` (founder grounding)
- general party/founding rituals from RPGs and management intros

### Agent Town caveat
This is not just profile setup.
It is the creation of the first two minds of the company.

---

## 3.4 Sigil/Open — trust and alignment gate

### Product purpose
Turn the pair from “named” into “aligned.”

### Player meaning
- We proved we can operate together.

### What changes after success
- the pair earns the right to create the founding crest
- House creation becomes justified

### What must be visible
- the point of the ritual
- clear completion state
- obvious next action

### What must not dominate
- unexplained ritual jargon
- fake celebratory bloat
- House/HQ messaging before completion

### Primary benchmark donors
- narrative ritual gates generally

### Agent Town caveat
This is not only symbolic.
It is the operational trust gate for the pair.

---

## 3.5 `/create` — crest / seal / founding artifact

### Product purpose
Create the first shared artifact of the company.

### Player meaning
- We made our company crest.
- We created something that marks this business as ours.

### What changes after success
- House creation becomes unlocked
- the company gains its first symbol/save-worthy artifact

### What must be visible
- playful but meaningful artifact creation
- short path to completion
- clear handoff to HQ

### What must not dominate
- hidden technical complexity
- vague explanation of why the step matters

### Primary benchmark donors
- founding/crafting rituals generally

### Agent Town caveat
This artifact is both emotional and operational.
It is part of the company’s early durable identity.

---

## 3.6 House first entry — headquarters becomes real

### Product purpose
Make the House feel like the company’s HQ.

### Player meaning
- This is where our company lives.

### What changes after success
- the company has a true home base
- first mission can begin
- memory, workshop, mailroom, and progression spaces become legible

### What must be visible
- HQ framing
- the first relevant rooms only
- one obvious next action: first mission

### What must not dominate
- deep admin surfaces
- too many rooms at once
- future staffing complexity

### Primary benchmark donors
- `Software Inc.`
- `Big Ambitions`
- House Office clarity work from Agent Town branches

### Agent Town caveat
House is not just décor.
It is HQ + memory + workshop + operating shell.

---

## 3.7 First mission — first real work together

### Product purpose
Prove the pair can do one meaningful piece of work.

### Recommended first mission
- **Web Ops / market research mission**

### Player meaning
- We did our first real company task together.

### What changes after success
- the company has output
- the company has something worth remembering
- progression becomes deserved

### What must be visible
- clear mission brief
- clear success condition
- a visible result/evidence of work

### What must not dominate
- giant mission catalogs
- scary operator surfaces
- irrelevant parallel systems

### Primary benchmark donors
- `Game Dev Tycoon`
- `Startup Company`
- `while True: learn()`

### Agent Town caveat
This mission should use real platform primitives and point toward real-world value, not only tutorial points.

---

## 3.8 First memory save — company memory begins

### Product purpose
Capture the first durable piece of company knowledge.

### Player meaning
- We saved something that matters.
- The company now remembers.

### What changes after success
- first durable company memory exists
- the Library becomes meaningful immediately
- the pair gains a real sense of continuity

### What should count as valid first memory
At least one of:

1. mission result
2. note/artifact extracted from the mission
3. meaningful user-agent discussion that shaped the mission or business direction
4. later: captured config/mind state as a save-point-like artifact

### What must be visible
- “save to company memory” as a clear action
- simple destination/default shelf
- visible meaning of the save

### What must not dominate
- library admin sprawl
- publishing/review/safety complexity on first run

### Primary benchmark donors
- knowledge-heavy world games for curation feeling
- Agent Town Library itself as the unique substrate

### Agent Town caveat
The user-agent conversation is the seed memory of the company.
This is a major category difference.

---

## 3.9 Next quest reveal — world unlocks forward

### Product purpose
Point the pair toward the next concrete step.

### Player meaning
- We know what to do next.
- The world is opening because we earned it.

### What changes after success
- the next quest/room/capability becomes visible
- the player sees the company becoming more real

### What must be visible
- one clear next quest
- plain-language reason it is now unlocked
- visible relationship to what the pair just accomplished

### What must not dominate
- giant dashboards
- too many branching options at once
- late-game systems prematurely unlocked

### Primary benchmark donors
- `Game Dev Tycoon`
- `Game Dev Story`
- good quest-log pacing generally

### Agent Town caveat
Unlocks should map to:
- real readiness,
- real memory,
- real trust,
- real capability.

Not fake XP alone.

These should not dominate ZHC0 but should inform naming and IA now.

### 4.1 Library

- artifact shelf
- share/import/publish surface
- later ZHC0-to-ZHC0 exchange surface

### 4.2 Registry
Later role:
- artifact market
- package identity
- proof/discovery layer

### 4.3 Worker shelf / Office
Later role:
- helper deployment
- staffing
- office specialization
- company scaling

### 4.4 Pony
Later role:
- mailroom
- company-to-company coordination rail
- customer/supplier/internal dispatch

### 4.5 Tracks
Later role:
- unlocking rooms, workers, market-facing surfaces, and higher autonomy

---

## 5. The reproducibility rule

Each of the screens above must be reproducible through an artifact chain:

1. narrative/product spec
2. formal state machine / TLA+ artifact
3. UI projection spec
4. TDD roadmap/contract
5. executable tests
6. screenshot evidence

If any screen cannot be reconstructed from that chain, the work is under-documented.

---

## 6. Immediate implementation order

The practical order remains:

1. `/start`
2. first worker setup
3. Town Hall
4. sigil/open
5. `/create`
6. House first entry
7. first mission
8. first memory save
9. next quest reveal

This keeps the founders loop intact and playable as soon as possible.

# Founders Plot V1.4.2 Patch 2 — HQ Progression Art Direction

## Purpose

HQ upgrades are one of the most important emotional reward moments in Founders Plot. The player must feel that the town grew.

Patch 2 requires real, gameplay-scale visual progression for HQ levels 1, 3, and 5.

## Style lock

Use the accepted V1.4.2 Agent Town frontier-storybook / soft 3D collectible art baseline.

Do not create a new art direction.

## HQ Level 1 — Claim Cabin / Starter Office

Visual read:

- humble first HQ;
- small footprint;
- simple cabin, claim office, or wagon-cabin hybrid;
- rougher materials;
- one modest sign/mailbox/crate;
- low, compact silhouette.

Gameplay emotion:

> “This is where the settlement begins.”

## HQ Level 3 — Expanded Homestead / Civic Office

Visual read:

- wider footprint or added wing;
- stronger porch/awning;
- improved roof and exterior polish;
- visible town-planning props: notice board, map table, crates, lamp, barrel;
- mid-tier civic identity.

Gameplay emotion:

> “The settlement is becoming a real civic operation.”

## HQ Level 5 — Frontier Town Hall

Visual read:

- largest and most formal HQ silhouette;
- civic tower, bell, flag, crest, or signature roofline;
- polished entrance/steps;
- public-facing sign and banners;
- unmistakable town-hall identity.

Gameplay emotion:

> “This is now the heart of a town.”

## Required visual differences

At gameplay scale, L1/L3/L5 must differ by at least three of these:

- footprint width;
- silhouette height;
- roofline shape;
- civic props;
- entrance treatment;
- tower/bell/flag presence;
- material finish;
- surrounding micro-props.

Color/trim alone is not enough.

## Required proofs

1. File hashes differ.
2. Browser-canvas visual delta passes.
3. Gameplay-scale gallery screenshot passes review.
4. No-label gallery screenshot still shows clear progression.

## Prompt storage

Any generated or edited HQ asset must have a prompt file under:

```text
specs/prompts/v1_4_2_patch_2/
public/experiences/founders-plot/assets/prompts/v1_4_2_patch_2/
```

The prompt file is part of the asset source and must be referenced in the asset manifest.

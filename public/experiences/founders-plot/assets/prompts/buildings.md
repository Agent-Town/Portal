# Building And Object Prompts

## hq-levels

Create a small frontier headquarters set for a cozy civic town-builder. Wooden porch, warm windows, brass trim, map rail, simple roof. Each higher level should feel more established, but remain readable at small size.

## lumber-camp

Create a cozy frontier Lumber Camp with stacked logs, a saw bench, tiny shed, safe hand tools, and warm sunlit wood tones. Friendly rounded silhouette.

## farm-plot

Create a compact frontier Farm Plot with tilled rows, sprouting crops, a water barrel, and warm soil. Friendly and readable at small size.

## quarry

Create a small frontier Quarry with pale stone piles, a pulley frame, small cart, and safe tools. Warm civic-builder mood.

## workshop

Create a frontier Workshop with wood and brass details, chimney, crates, and a helpful practical silhouette for a cozy town-builder.

## market-stall

Create a small frontier Market Stall with canvas awning, baskets, crates, and welcoming civic market energy.

## contract-board

Create a physical town Contract Board: wooden posts, parchment sheets, brass pins, and a lantern. No readable text.

## welcome-sign

Create a cozy public-square Welcome Sign centerpiece with flower barrel, path stones, lanterns, and civic warmth. No readable text.

## foreman-hut

Create a small Foreman Hut for a frontier town-builder with a map table, porch lantern, rolled plans, and subtle teal intelligence accents.

## lots

Create two lot markers:

- a buildable lot with stakes, packed soil, and hopeful construction markers;
- a locked lot with chained stakes and a muted keep-out marker.

# World Grid Goals

## V5.0-V5.5 Territory Awareness And Controlled Sandbox

Help the player understand:

- where the home town sits;
- which nearby cells are visible, locked, or claimable;
- what terrain means in plain language;
- when public presence is opt-in and public-safe;
- why civic service advice cannot mutate the world by itself;
- how public works contributions are previewed, capped, and idempotent;
- how sandbox free play stays typed, moderated, rollback-safe, and separate
  from private town state;
- why unrestricted free-play systems are not available yet.

Success is bounded expansion literacy and safe experimentation, not
unrestricted world play.

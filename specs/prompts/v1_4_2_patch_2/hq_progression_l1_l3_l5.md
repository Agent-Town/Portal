# GPT Image 2 Prompt — HQ Progression L1 / L3 / L5

## Purpose

Generate or edit three distinct HQ building assets for Agent Town: Founders Plot. These assets must match the accepted V1.4.2 warm frontier storybook / soft 3D collectible style and read clearly at gameplay scale.

## Global style

Warm frontier civic-builder game art, soft 3D storybook collectible style, cozy dusty morning light, hand-authored game asset quality, rounded forms, readable silhouette, charming but not childish, frontier materials, warm wood, canvas, brass, stone, gentle shadows, clear game-object silhouette, no text labels, no logos, no UI, no cyberpunk, no pixel art, no photorealism.

## Asset 1: HQ Level 1

Create a small starter headquarters for a frontier settlement.

Required read:
- humble claim cabin or wagon-office hybrid;
- small footprint;
- simple porch or step;
- rough timber/canvas details;
- one modest sign/mailbox/crate;
- low compact roofline;
- clearly the beginning of a settlement.

Avoid: tower, grand civic hall, large footprint, polished town-hall look.

## Asset 2: HQ Level 3

Create a visibly upgraded mid-tier headquarters.

Required read:
- expanded homestead / civic office;
- wider footprint or side extension;
- improved porch/awning;
- stronger roof treatment;
- town-planning props such as map table, notice board, crates, lantern, water barrel;
- noticeably more organized and civic than Level 1.

Avoid: only recoloring Level 1; full town-hall grandeur.

## Asset 3: HQ Level 5

Create the current final-tier Founders Plot headquarters.

Required read:
- proper frontier town hall;
- largest and most formal silhouette;
- civic tower, bell, flag, crest, or distinctive roofline;
- polished public entrance, steps, banners, signboard without legible text;
- clearly the heart of a real town.

Avoid: looking like a small cabin; identical footprint to L1/L3.

## Negative prompt

No readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no modern city hall, no guns, no harsh cowboy parody, no saloon as HQ, no cyberpunk, no neon, no flat icon style, no pixel art, no photorealistic render, no cluttered background.

## Production notes

- Generate on clean simple background suitable for later background removal or crop.
- Assets may need post-processing because GPT Image 2 may not output production transparency directly.
- Keep lighting and perspective consistent across all three.
- Store final assets as WebP under `public/experiences/founders-plot/assets/buildings/`.
